About AZL Digital

AZL Digital Language Institute is committed to helping students develop language skills that create real opportunities for their future. The institute focuses on practical learning, career preparation, and international communication.

Founder & Instructor

SH Zahid Ahmed

SH Zahid Ahmed is the founder and lead instructor of AZL Digital. With more than six years of experience in language education, he has successfully trained numerous students in international language programs.

His teaching philosophy focuses on clarity, student confidence, and practical communication. He believes language learning should not only be theoretical, but also applicable in real-life situations.

Through dedication, discipline, and professional instruction, he continues to guide students toward achieving their academic and career goals internationally.
